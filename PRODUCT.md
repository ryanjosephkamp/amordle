# Awordle Product

## Name

Awordle, pronounced “Award-le”.

## Purpose

Awordle combines classic single-puzzle OG play with five-puzzle GO chains across Daily, Practice, and authenticated Multiplayer experiences. Players should be able to enter a game quickly, understand its state immediately, submit guesses without interface friction, and trust that progress and results remain correct across navigation, refresh, sign-in, and device changes.

## Users

Awordle serves casual and committed word-game players on desktop and mobile browsers. Players may use guest/local play, sign in for account-scoped progress, return to unfinished Solo games, compete asynchronously, inspect results and definitions, and review history, progression, and ranking information.

## Product principles

1. Gameplay state is clearer than decoration.
2. Canonical gameplay, persistence, privacy, and multiplayer contracts remain trustworthy.
3. Mobile play and accessibility are first-class workflows.
4. Motion and sound communicate state rather than distract from it.
5. Presentation may evolve only after functional-clone parity is independently accepted.

## Current boundary

This initial repository is a source-level successor foundation. Independent Supabase/Vercel provisioning, migration application, authority-enabled E2E, hosted manual review, functional-clone acceptance, and frontend redesign remain future gated work.
