# amordle Product

## Name

amordle, pronounced like “immortal”.

## Purpose

amordle combines classic single-puzzle OG play with five-puzzle GO chains across Daily, Practice, and authenticated Multiplayer experiences. Players should be able to enter a game quickly, understand its state immediately, submit guesses without interface friction, and trust that progress and results remain correct across navigation, refresh, sign-in, and device changes.

## Users

amordle serves casual and committed word-game players on desktop and mobile browsers. Players may use guest/local play, sign in for account-scoped progress, return to unfinished Solo games, compete asynchronously, inspect results and definitions, and review history, progression, and ranking information.

## Product principles

1. Gameplay state is clearer than decoration.
2. Canonical gameplay, persistence, privacy, and multiplayer contracts remain trustworthy.
3. Mobile play and accessibility are first-class workflows.
4. Motion and sound communicate state rather than distract from it.
5. Research, inspiration analysis, and concept exploration may precede functional-clone acceptance, but production presentation may change only after functional-clone parity is independently accepted.

## Current boundary

This repository is a source-level successor foundation with a dedicated Vercel deployment and an unconfigured dedicated Supabase target. Migration application, authority-enabled E2E, hosted manual review, functional-clone acceptance, and frontend redesign implementation remain future gated work. Design research and concept work may proceed without changing production source or dependencies.
