Feedback preference authority

Avatar Storage authority

The second migration creates one public bucket named amordle-public-avatars-v1. Public reads are intentional because profile images are public. Authenticated owners alone may insert, update, or delete. Objects are capped at 6 MiB, limited to PNG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF, and named with random UUID v4 paths under avatars/. SVG and arbitrary binary uploads are excluded.

The application additionally verifies magic bytes, MIME agreement, dimensions, and megapixels; re-encodes still images to strip metadata; preserves animated GIFs; and keeps the existing public HTTPS URL option. A collision with an incompatible bucket fails the migration instead of rewriting provider configuration.
