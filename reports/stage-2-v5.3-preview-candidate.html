Implemented outcome

Untouched letter keys now use a raised blue-gray surface. Ruled-out letters use a near-black surface, light text, and a visible ×. Correct, present, absent, and removed evidence retains domain precedence and does not rely on color alone.

Both COMBAT participants now see keyboard evidence derived from the same current-puzzle chronological board. GO resets at a puzzle transition and rebuilds from current moves and rescored seeds.
