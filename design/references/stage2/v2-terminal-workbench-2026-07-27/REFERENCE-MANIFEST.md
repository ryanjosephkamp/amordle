## Recommendation

Approve **Quiet Workbench** as the governing Stage 2 direction.

It makes the terminal identity structural rather than decorative:

- compact command and context rails;
- titled, ruled work regions instead of generic cards;
- stable character-aware alignment;
- persistent human-readable status;
- shared board, keyboard, evidence, and action anatomy;
- equal-quality cool light and charcoal-blue dark themes;
- mobile translation through stacked regions and 44px controls.

The product remains a familiar browser application. Players do not type shell
commands or learn terminal vocabulary.

## Binding interpretation

If approved, bind:

- the calm terminal-workbench thesis;
- the compact typography roles and spacing rhythm;
- the command/context/status rail hierarchy;
- the low-radius, one-rule component system;
- the light/dark palette strategy;
- the non-color evidence anatomy;
- the desktop/mobile composition rules;
- the player-facing editorial direction in
  [`copy-register.md`](./copy-register.md);
- the component anatomy in
  [`component-anatomy.md`](./component-anatomy.md).

Do not bind:

- generated words, dates, names, balances, scores, ratings, timers, ranks, XP,
  or counts;
- raster icons or exact pixel placement;
- any generated text that conflicts with the copy register;
- any unsupported statistic, field, definition, feature, or interaction;
- any rule, route, API, service, or persisted behavior.

The 237 clauses and higher authorities always win.

## Material corrections

- OG always contains one puzzle. GO uses 5, 7, or 10 in Practice and 5 in
  Daily.
- A GO seed is labeled `SEED EVIDENCE`; it is not a player guess.
- Calendar and Marketplace use in-game coins. Binding confirmation copy is
  “No coins are spent until you confirm.”
- Word Explorer must use the implemented definition behavior. Generated
  dictionary entries are layout samples, not data authority.
- COMBAT is asynchronous alternating-turn play. Generated “real-time” wording
  is rejected.
- Loading uses structural skeleton rows; the raster spinner is not binding.
- Universal icons are illustrative. Implementation remains text-first with
  accessible names.
