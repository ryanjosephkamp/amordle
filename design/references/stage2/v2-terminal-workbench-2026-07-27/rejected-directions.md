# Stage 2 v2 Direction Critique

The three explorations were reviewed at native size against the product
contract and the Impeccable product-interface rubric.

Scores use 0–4 for coherence, hierarchy, density, spacing, typography,
interaction completeness, responsive translation, copy, accessibility
potential, and terminal authenticity. Approval requires every category at
least 3, total at least 34/40, terminal authenticity 4, and no forbidden
anti-pattern.

## Quiet Workbench — recommended

- Score: **37/40**
- Strengths: clear TUI structure without theater; strong active-task
  hierarchy; restrained light/dark themes; usable mobile translation; compact
  commands and persistent status; evidence system survives without color.
- Corrections carried into the approval set: fewer decorative icons, explicit
  OG/GO counts, deterministic copy register, no generated data authority.
- Decision: recommend as the one governing direction.

## Dense Match Console — rejected as a complete direction

- Score: **31/40**
- Strengths: symmetrical COMBAT panes, excellent rapid scanning, strong table
  density, convincing terminal authenticity.
- Failures: dark-only intensity, cramped small text, weak mobile breathing
  room, excessive console density for general players, and too little
  distinction between primary and secondary data.
- Retained structural lesson: symmetrical participant panes and compact ruled
  data tables.
- Decision: provenance only; do not implement its palette or global density.

## Inline Command Desk — rejected as a complete direction

- Score: **34/40**, but terminal authenticity scored 3.
- Strengths: excellent Calendar inspector, open ruled composition, readable
  account/data hierarchy, strong light-theme restraint.
- Failures: drifts toward editorial productivity software, uses unsupported
  fields and statistics, and turns “Command:” into a decorative repeated
  device.
- Retained structural lesson: open Calendar/data regions and stable label
  columns.
- Decision: provenance only; do not implement its unsupported data or repeated
  command captions.

The approval boards use one Quiet Workbench system. Lessons from rejected
directions are structural only and do not create a hybrid visual authority.
