## Binding interpretation

- Use Lichess for information architecture, Lichess source for structural
  research only, and macOS Terminal/Grok Build for mood only.
- Preserve system-neutral surfaces, charcoal ink, sky-teal accent under ten
  percent, semantic green/amber/slate evidence, Geist prose, and Geist Mono
  boards/data.
- Keep the board, keyboard, controls, tables, and icons code-native. No raster
  asset pass is proposed.
- Prefer open rails, one-pixel separators, and minimal structural panels over
  card grids.
- Preserve 44px targets, visible focus, 200% reflow, reduced motion, forced
  colors, and mobile layouts without horizontal document overflow.
