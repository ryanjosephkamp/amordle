## Purpose and authority

This is the durable visual authority for the greenfield amordle implementation. Read it before planning architecture, styling, responsive behavior, route composition, or implementation milestones.

It records:

- the final locked image for every accepted desktop and mobile surface;
- the binding description and correction notes that override generated-image drift;
- the relationship between product routes and concept coverage;
- the implementation rules that prevent generated fixtures or art defects from becoming product behavior.

Current user instructions, `CONSTITUTION.md`, `FUNCTIONAL-CONTRACT.md`, `BACKEND-AND-SERVICES-CONTRACT.md`, and `TESTING-AND-ACCEPTANCE-CONTRACT.md` remain higher-authority behavior sources. A generated image is a visual target, not independent authority for product rules, data, copy, privacy, or service state. The binding notes below are the portable replacement for the excluded historical selection-gate files.

## Status key

- **Locked:** Ryan selected the image. Its resolved selection gate and binding corrections are part of the lock.
- **Binding note:** A textual correction or product rule that overrides misleading, impossible, inaccessible, fabricated, or legally risky details in generated art.
- **Fixture:** Illustrative content only; production values must derive from real state and sanctioned data.
