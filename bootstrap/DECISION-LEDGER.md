## Contradiction rule

The current user request and constitution override this ledger. Otherwise:

1. preserve functional behavior required by the locked shell;
2. preserve backend/privacy constraints required by the 45 migrations;
3. discard shell or old-Amordle presentation decisions;
4. choose the simpler implementation that remains testable and complete;
5. record any material new decision before implementation.
