      required to see all six board rows and the complete keyboard.

- [ ] Rotate the phone and confirm the landscape board/keyboard layout remains
      usable.

      not pull you away from manual review.
- [ ] Use `Latest row` and confirm follow-newest behavior resumes.
- [ ] Check physical keyboard and on-screen keyboard input.
- [ ] Check Evidence and game tools disclosure on a phone.
